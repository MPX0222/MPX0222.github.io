Welcome to My Homepage!

I'm now a researcher at Paradoox AI Research. I have completed my M.Phil. degree in Data Science and Analytics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), where I was advised by Prof. Yuyu Luo and Prof. Nan Tang. Prior to that, I graduated from Jinan University with a B.Eng. degree in Intelligence Science and Technology in 2023. In 2025, I interned at the International Digital Economy Academy, under the supervision of Dr. Chengjin Xu and Prof. Jian Guo.

What I am doing?

At there, I continue to strive to bring AI into practical application in real-world scenarios, by developing simple but effective methodologies. Specifically, my current research interests comprise: AI for Database (e.g., NL2SQL, Evaluation of DB LLMs), AI for Finance (e.g., Financial LLM Benchmarking, Next-Generation Agentic Finance), Reasonable and Efficient Agent System (e.g., Cross-Env Agentic Reasoning, Latent Space Agentic Frameworks), Large Language Models (e.g., Reinforcement Learning, Diffusion Language Models). Overall, I keep open for opportunities of collaboration. Feel free to contact me if you are interested in these research topics.
